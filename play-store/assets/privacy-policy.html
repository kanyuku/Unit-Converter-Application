Unit Converter Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Information and use

Unit Converter processes measurements entered by you on your device to calculate length, weight, and volume conversions. The app does not collect, share, or transmit personal data. Conversion values are not stored by the app.

The app does not request device permissions and does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party SDKs. An account and an internet connection are not required.
